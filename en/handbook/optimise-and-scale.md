# Optimise and scale

**For:** Advertisers and media buyers with at least one week of campaign data.

Once a campaign has run for about a week, you have enough data to improve it. Optimise first, then scale what works.

## 1. When a campaign is ready to scale

Scale a campaign when all of these are true:

- ✅ It has run for at least **7 days**
- ✅ Its CPA in the **Cohort** view is at or below your target <!-- TODO (Product): same day-7 cohort maturity question as first-week.md -->
- ✅ It spends most of its daily budget each day
- ✅ At least one creative clearly outperforms the others

If CPA is above target, optimise before you scale.

## 2. Optimise

Work through these in order. Change one thing, wait 2–3 days, then check again.

| Lever | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Creatives** | Pause the weakest sets. Add 2–3 new ones based on your best performer: same offer, new visual or message |
| **Offer** | If every creative has a high CPA, the offer may be the problem. Test a different bonus or promotion |
| **Landing page** | If CTR is good but registrations are low, test a different pre-lander template or a shorter form |
| **Geos** | Split out geos with very different CPAs into their own campaigns, so each can have its own bid |
| **Bid** | If CPA is well below target but volume is low, raise the bid by 10–20% |

## 3. Scale

- **Raise the daily budget gradually**, about 20–30% every 2–3 days. Large jumps can reset learning.
- **Keep the budget at about 100× your bid.** If you raise the bid, raise the budget too.
- **Add geos** in new campaigns, rather than adding them to a campaign that works, so you can compare results.
- **Try a second ad format** with the same offer. Formats reach different players.
- **Keep your wallet ahead of spend.** Ask your Account Manager to top up before you raise budgets.

## 4. Move to a deeper goal

When your Register campaigns are stable, move closer to revenue:

1. Make sure deposits are tracked through **S2S** or **Partner Postback** ([Connect tracking](/en/tracking/overview#_2-2-which-system-holds-your-conversion-data)).
2. Create a new campaign with **FTD** as the target event.
3. Use **Audience** to retarget players who registered but did not deposit. See [Audience → Segments](/en/audience/segments).
