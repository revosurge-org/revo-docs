## Getting started

### How long does it take to launch? {#how-long-does-it-take-to-launch}

If you only need the Web Tracker, you can launch the same day. Deposit tracking through S2S takes about 2–5 days of developer time. See [Start here → How it works](./#_1-how-it-works).

### Do I need a developer? {#do-i-need-a-developer}

Yes, to install the Web Tracker on your site (about 30 minutes). Partner Postback needs no code.

### Can I add my team? {#can-i-add-my-team}

Not yet. Each account has one login email. Use a shared work address if several people run campaigns.

## Wallet and billing

### How do I add funds? {#how-do-i-add-funds}

Contact your Account Manager. There is no self-serve payment yet. See [Account and wallet → Fund your wallet](./account-and-wallet#_2-fund-your-wallet).

### Are deposits refundable? {#are-deposits-refundable}

No. Deposit what you plan to spend in your test period. <!-- TODO: confirm policy -->

### What happens when my balance runs out? {#what-happens-when-my-balance-runs-out}

Your campaigns stop delivering until you top up. See [Keep your balance topped up](./account-and-wallet#_3-keep-your-balance-topped-up).

## Tracking

### Why is my Product still Inactive? {#why-is-my-product-still-inactive}

The Web Tracker has not sent its first event. Check the script is on your site with the correct Tracker ID, then visit the site and watch the **Event Stream**.

### Why can't I select my Product in Create Campaign? {#why-can-t-i-select-my-product-in-create-campaign}

Only Active Products can be selected. Finish [tracking setup](/en/tracking/overview) first.

### I already have players. Will my first-deposit numbers be wrong? {#i-already-have-players-will-my-first-deposit-numbers-be-wrong}

They can be. When tracking starts, an existing player's next deposit looks like a first deposit. Send historical deposits first, or agree a cut-off date with your Account Manager. See [Core concepts → First deposits](/en/tracking/core-concepts#_5-first-deposits).

## Campaigns

### Which target event should I choose first? {#which-target-event-should-i-choose-first}

**Register**, unless deposits are already tracked reliably. See [Create your campaign → Objective and ad format](./create-campaign#_2-objective-and-ad-format).

### Which ad format should I start with? {#which-ad-format-should-i-start-with}

**Pop** is the fastest to launch because it needs only a landing page.

### How much should I spend per day? {#how-much-should-i-spend-per-day}

About 100× your bid (at least 50×). See [Create your campaign → Bid and daily budget](./create-campaign#_4-bid-and-daily-budget).

### Is there a review before my campaign goes live? {#is-there-a-review-before-my-campaign-goes-live}

No. It starts at the scheduled time after you click **Launch**. <!-- TODO (Product): same Launch / Campaign Summary question as create-campaign.md -->

### My campaign isn't spending. What should I check? {#my-campaign-isn-t-spending-what-should-i-check}

See [Your first week → Diagnose common problems](./first-week#_3-diagnose-common-problems).

### Why do my Analytics numbers keep changing? {#why-do-my-analytics-numbers-keep-changing}

The Cohort view credits conversions up to 14 days after the click, so recent numbers keep growing until they mature.
