## Your launch path

Complete steps 1–5 before launch. Prepare tracking, landing pages and creatives in parallel where possible. For AppsFlyer, complete configuration before launch and verify attribution/postbacks after the campaign generates real installs and events.

| Step | Section | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Account and wallet](/en/handbook/account-and-wallet) | Create your account, fund your wallet and plan top-ups. |
| 2 | [Connect tracking](/en/tracking/overview) | Set up your Product, choose your tracking sources and follow their verification steps. |
| 3 | [Landing page & conversion path](/en/handbook/landing-page) | Prepare the destination and the path from click to conversion. |
| 4 | [Creatives](/en/handbook/creatives) | Prepare ads that fit your format, market and offer. |
| 5 | [Create your campaign](/en/handbook/create-campaign) | Choose your goal, targeting, bid, budget and schedule, then review and launch. |
