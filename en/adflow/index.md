# Start here: Launch your first ad placement

This handbook is for publishers monetizing a website. To launch your first placement, set it up, integrate it, and verify requests, rendered ads, and reporting data.

## First launch checklist

- [1. Create an account, site, and ad placement](./create-placement) · Set up your account, site, and placement, then get the code or integration parameters.
- [2. Choose an integration method](./choose-integration) · Choose a guide based on your website’s ad stack.
- [3. Verify and launch](./verify-and-launch) · Check requests, rendering, and reporting on the deployed page.
- [4. Review reports and earnings](./reporting) · Review placement performance, earnings, and billing after launch.
- [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting) · Troubleshoot installation, rendering, or reporting based on what you observe.
