## First launch checklist

<div class="adflow-table" role="region" aria-label="First launch checklist" tabindex="0">

| Step | What to do | How to tell it’s done |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1. Create an account, site, and ad placement](./create-placement) | Set up your account, site, and placement, then get the code or integration parameters. | You have a placement and its code or integration parameters. |
| [2. Choose an integration method](./choose-integration) | Choose a guide based on your website’s ad stack. | The target page sends a request for the placement. |
| [3. Verify and launch](./verify-and-launch) | Check requests, rendering, and reporting on the deployed page. | A test ad renders and the data appears in the report. |
