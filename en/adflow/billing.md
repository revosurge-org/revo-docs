# View bills, update payment details, and reconcile monthly earnings

> Planned for this release. Use these instructions once the corresponding features are live.

<p>Publishers responsible for earnings and payments can go to Account Settings → Billing in AdFlow to check daily receivables and monthly totals, and to view or update payment details.</p>
<h2 id="earnings-and-receivables">Review earnings and receivables</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Recorded earnings this month</strong> is the total of daily receivables recorded in the current calendar month.</li>
<li><strong>Total recorded earnings</strong> is the sum of all earnings recorded to date, including this month.</li>
<li>In Receivables, select a billing month to see daily receivables and the monthly total. Use these figures for reconciliation and invoicing.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="update-payment-details">Update payment details</h2>
<ol>
<li>In Billing Details, check the billing email and registered payment methods. The billing email comes from the email address used to register.</li>
<li>Select Edit and enter bank details or USDT wallet details as needed. You can register both methods.</li>
<li>Save and verify the details shown. For a wallet, use the public receiving address on the corresponding network.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="monthly-reconciliation-and-payment">Monthly reconciliation and payment</h2>
<p>AdFlow settles monthly outside the platform. To reconcile a bill or confirm payment arrangements, contact your account manager with the billing month and receivable amount. If the receiving account on the invoice differs, confirm it with your account manager using your registered official email address.</p>
<h2 id="done">How to tell you’re done</h2>
<p>You can select the correct billing month, verify daily receivables and the monthly total, and confirm the payment details match the arrangement agreed outside the platform.</p>
