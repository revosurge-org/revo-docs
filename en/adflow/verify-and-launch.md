# Verify and launch

1. Trigger a test placement on the page where the code is deployed. Check the account and placement IDs in the request.
2. Confirm that an ad renders in the expected location. For GAM, check the ad unit, line item, and creative.
3. Check the report for the test period and placement. When comparing with GAM, align time zones, ad units, and metrics.
