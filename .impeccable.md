## Design Context

### Users
RevoSurge Docs (docs.revosurge.com) serves new self-serve advertisers first: business owners, marketers and media buyers launching their first AdWave campaign without a BD call. It also serves developers installing tracking, publishers integrating AdFlow, and SSPs. Pages are read in English, Simplified Chinese (cn) and Traditional Chinese for Hong Kong (hk). Readers are usually mid-task, often with the AdWave or DataPulse UI open beside the docs, scanning for one specific answer, or forwarding a page to a developer. The job: get from sign-up to a live campaign, and find the answer to "what do I do now?" fast.

### Brand Personality
Clear, practical, confident. Plain words, short sentences, active voice, no hype or marketing fluff. The reader should come away feeling "I can do this myself", and should find answers quickly.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Refine the VitePress default theme rather than replacing it. Keep the indigo brand scale (`--vp-c-brand-*`), Inter for UI and body, and support both light and dark themes.
- Build on the site's own custom patterns: tinted uppercase table headers, the "In this article" card (`<nav class="article-toc">`) and the locale code pill in the nav.
- Never hide content behind collapsible toggles. Answers stay visible, searchable and linkable. Don't use `::: details` for new content.
- No specific external reference site. Use judgement within the existing site style.
- Anti-references: decorative gradients, glassmorphism, cards wrapped around everything, and hidden or accordion content.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA by default (contrast, visible focus, semantic headings).

### Design Principles
1. **Answers in plain sight.** Don't collapse or hide content. Every section and question gets its own heading and anchor, so it shows in the outline and can be linked.
2. **Structure before styling.** Reach for headings, tables and lists that VitePress already styles well. Add CSS only for a pattern that repeats across the site, and only with theme tokens.
3. **Scannable first.** Lead with the question or label, keep one idea per paragraph, and make the hierarchy obvious at a glance.
4. **Same shape in every language.** en, cn and hk pages share structure and heading IDs (explicit `{#id}` in cn/hk), so links and anchors work in all three.
5. **Quiet by default, one accent.** Brand colour is for links and actions, not decoration. Everything must hold up in light and dark themes.
